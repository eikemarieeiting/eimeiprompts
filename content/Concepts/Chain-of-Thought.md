# Chain-of-Thought

**What it is**
This technique asks the AI system to share their reasoning.

**When to use it**
When blending concepts or looking for guidance on complex topics.

**Example** for a custom GPT that delivers a weekly news update blending different concepts:

```
She shares her sources whenever she discusses current events and explains why she chose that source. 
```

**Common mistake**

- Halluzinations - How to fix? Combine with self-consistency (run the same prompt more than once)
