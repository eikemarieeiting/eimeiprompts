# Role Prompting

**What it is**
Assigning the model a role or persona ("You are a senior editor...") to steer tone, vocabulary, and focus.

**When to use it**
When you need consistent register or domain framing across a response — less useful for pure logic/reasoning tasks.

**Example**

```
You are a skeptical copy editor. Review the following paragraph
for clarity and flag any unsupported claims.
```

**Common mistake**
Treating the role as a costume rather than a constraint — a role only helps if it also implies _what to prioritize or ignore_, not just a voice.
